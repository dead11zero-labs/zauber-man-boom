Meshes/                  334 models in .glb, one file per model
                         LOD levels and collision shapes are generated by Godot
                         on import; six decorative models ship without collision,
                         as in the original pack

Materials/               21 materials on four atlas variants
                           Metro_new.tres            clean walls and props
                           Metro_old.tres            worn variant
                           Metro_exodus.tres         cold blue tunnel set
                           Metro_Alpha.tres          cut-out variant (fences, grilles)
                           MetroPorcelain.tres       tiled surfaces, machines
                           Metro_clean_emiss_*.tres  neon signs and EXIT boards
                                                     (blue, green, purple, red,
                                                     white, yellow)
                           Metro2_emmision_*.tres    advert panels and screens
                           Metro_Alpha_Emiss_*.tres  lit cut-out signs
                           Yellow_Light.tres         lamp glass
                           ground_scene_assets.tres  ground of the layout scene

Textures/                5 textures
                           PixelArt---EA01-Metro_Diff_clean.png    2048x2048
                           PixelArt---EA01-Metro_Diff_dirt.png     2048x2048

Prefabs/                 382 scenes, grouped by station part
                           Arch/  Fence/  Ground/  GroundDecor/  LCD/
                           RoofDecor/  TechRoom/  Vehicle/  WallDecor/

Scenes/                  5 scenes, each with its own camera
   Demo.tscn             9382 nodes, 2162 meshes, 2120 collision bodies,
                         261 lights (183 casting shadows)
   Demo2026.tscn        10072 nodes, 2301 meshes, 2252 collision bodies,
                         407 lights (368 casting shadows)
   Shooter.tscn          3855 nodes,  877 meshes,  858 collision bodies,
                         169 lights (136 casting shadows)
   Prolog.tscn            696 nodes,  147 meshes,  142 collision bodies,
                          22 lights (22 casting shadows)
   Assets.tscn           1952 nodes,  362 meshes,  351 collision bodies,
                          30 lights (17 casting shadows)

Everything is stored as data in the scene files. Nothing is generated at
run time, so the station looks the same in the editor as it does in game.
